# The Starling brand

## The mark — "the banking starling"

One blade-shaped starling, banking upward. Drawn as a single curved silhouette
(bird first, star second — the four sharp points are the echo of "star-ling"),
rising up-and-right: **under book → upside**. The diamond-sharp tips deliberately
rhyme with the ask-diamond on the board's depth bar — the brand mark and the
product instrument share one geometry.

- Master asset: `public/brand/mark.svg` (24 viewBox, butter on transparent)
- Favicon: `public/favicon.svg` (butter bird on the Terminal ground tile, rx 7)
- Inline component: `StarlingMark` in `app/layout.tsx`

## Lockup

`[mark] starling.` — mark in butter, wordmark lowercase Inter 800 at −0.03em,
period in butter. The mark leads at 21px in the masthead, 17px in the footer.
"powered by [lectr script]" sits beside it; lectr's handwritten mark is lectr's,
never restyled.

## Rules

1. **Butter `#e8dab6` is the brand ink.** On butter surfaces the bird flips to
   ground ink `#08090a`. Never gradient, never outlined, never rotated further.
2. **The bird always banks upward.** Never mirror it downward — a diving bird
   reads as a loss, and green/red stay reserved for real deltas.
3. One mark per view (the one-butter-lamp rule applies to the brand too):
   masthead lockup is the lamp; don't scatter extra birds around the page.
4. Minimum size 14px; below that use the favicon tile.
5. The wordmark is always lowercase `starling.` — the period is part of the name.

## Voice

Same register as the product: sentence case, numbers tabular, claims backed by
the book. Starling is the buy-side scout; lectr is the proof. "Powered by lectr"
appears wherever the brand does.
