# The Starling brand

## The logotype — one word, one mark

The mark and the word are a single unit: **the bird is the tittle of the i.**
"starling" is set lowercase (Inter 800, −0.03em) with a dotless ı, and the
banking starling hovers in the dot's place — a blade-shaped bird (bird first,
star second: the sharp points echo "star-ling") banking up-and-right:
**under book → upside**. Its diamond tips rhyme with the ask-diamond on the
board's depth bar — brand and instrument share one geometry.

- Logotype component: `StarlingLogotype` in `app/layout.tsx` (scales with font-size)
- Standalone bird: `public/brand/mark.svg` (for contexts where the word can't fit)
- Favicon: `public/favicon.svg` (butter bird on the Terminal ground tile, rx 7)

## Rules

1. **Butter `#e8dab6` is the brand ink** — the bird is the logotype's only
   accent; there is no trailing period, no second color. On butter surfaces the
   bird flips to ground ink `#08090a`.
2. **The bird always banks upward.** Never mirror it downward — a diving bird
   reads as a loss, and green/red stay reserved for real deltas.
3. One logotype per view (the one-butter-lamp rule applies to the brand):
   masthead is the lamp; don't scatter extra birds around the page.
4. The word is always lowercase **starling**. Never letterspace it, never set
   it in the serif; the bird never detaches to sit beside the word — integrated
   or standalone, never side-by-side.
5. Minimum logotype size 14px; below that use the favicon tile / standalone bird.

## Voice

Same register as the product: sentence case, numbers tabular, claims backed by
the book. Starling is the buy-side scout; lectr is the proof. "powered by
[lectr script]" sits beside the logotype; lectr's handwritten mark is lectr's,
never restyled.
